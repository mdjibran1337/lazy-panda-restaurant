Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | App: Lazy Panda Restaurant Partner

Welcome to Lazy Panda Restaurant Partner. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your restaurant's business data, personal details, and operational information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application and merchant dashboard.

1. Information We Collect

To provide seamless food delivery management, live order updates, and settlement tracking, we collect the following types of information:

  • Merchant & Business Details: Restaurant name, owner name, official email address, phone numbers, and physical business location/address.
  • Financial & Payout Data: Bank account details, UPI handles, and GST/Tax identification numbers required for processing your order revenues.
  • Operational Data: Menu items, prices, operating hours, kitchen preparation toggle statuses, and order acceptance/rejection logs.
  • Device & Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, and Firebase authentication tokens for push notifications regarding incoming live food orders.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following business purposes:

  • To process and route real-time food orders from customers directly to your kitchen dashboard.
  • To coordinate live deliveries with delivery executives and share essential dispatch tracking notes.
  • To process secure weekly or daily settlements and financial payouts into your registered bank accounts.
  • To send important service alerts, app performance updates, and instant notifications regarding new orders.

3. Data Sharing & Security

Your restaurant data is strictly protected. We do not sell or rent your business data to third parties. We share information only with:

  • Customers: Your restaurant name, address, menu items, and preparation status to facilitate ordering.
  • Delivery Partners: Pickup location details so drivers can collect prepared food packages.
  • Service Infrastructure: Secure cloud and authentication providers (such as Firebase and Supabase) that power our app backend under strict encryption standards.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your restaurant profile and transaction logs as long as your merchant account remains active with Lazy Panda. You can request account closure and data deletion anytime by contacting our partner support team through the app settings or via email.

5. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal modifications. Continued use of the Lazy Panda Restaurant Partner app after modifications indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data security, please reach out to us:

Email: support@lazypanda.com
App Support: Available 24x7 via the Help & Support section in your Lazy Panda Restaurant app.
